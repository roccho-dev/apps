## Source and comparison

The supported task starts with an existing named group and requests at most three new regions and one directed edge. Completion may add only missing instance candidates with exact display labels, supported kinds and consecutive occurrence indices. Graph operations, parents, identifiers and adoption remain in the existing legal/proof path.

Both arms use the same frozen source, canonical eight-part bundle, utterance and exact initial semantic graph/DecisionLog bytes. A still runs the original legal loop after a missing verdict, without completion. B may call TypeLLM once after an accepted missing verdict. Case and arm orders are fixed before output. Both arms use exact display labels without identity suffixes. No oracle may enter application or provider inputs.

One inventory call and at most eight Goal calls share the absolute dispatch deadline. W alone may call providers. Actual sends, including errors and unknown outcomes, count against separate cumulative limits of 300 Jev and 300 TypeLLM sends. Process allowance is reserved durably before launch. Unused allowance may be reconciled only after the owned process group is stopped and contiguous actual-send counters are read. The initial paired/browser phase reserves at most 199 Jev and 20 TypeLLM sends in addition to the prior one-send-per-provider smoke; at least 100 Jev sends remain for cause-led correction and confirmation.

## Main EFFECT thresholds

All conditions are required:

- In EACH arm, at least 9 of 10 inventory gates match the expected classification at accepted confidence, including all four missing cases. A matching low-confidence raw choice is not a successful gate.
- TypeLLM receives zero actual calls on sufficient and control cases.
- Both controls remain semantically unchanged in BOTH arms.
- B achieves exact final graphs on at least three of four sufficient cases and no fewer than A.
- B achieves exact final graphs on at least three of four missing cases, with at least two more successes than A.
- B supplies the minimal expected missing-instance candidate multiset in at least three of four missing cases.
- Source/artifact/runtime identity, counted actual sends, normal browser draw and Undo/Apply/reload evidence are independently supported.

An exact graph has the expected label/kind/count multiset, parent relationships and directed edges, with no unexpected semantic additions, removals or changes to pre-existing semantic fields. Identifier spelling and layout are not substitutes for these facts; layout and rendered containment are verified separately. Partial graphs are failures of the graph criterion, not partial successes.

## Correction, confirmation and verdicts

At most two corrections may follow identified causes. Tuning after exposed failures requires the separately sealed fresh four-case confirmation. No extra live round is required if the first frozen main round meets all EFFECT thresholds.

Fresh confirmation requires all four accepted gates correct in EACH arm, no TypeLLM call on its sufficient/control cases, zero control mutation in BOTH arms, B sufficient exact success with no regression versus A, both B missing graphs exact, both missing candidate completions minimal, and at least one gain over A. Main-round failures remain in the record and regressions must be resolved or explicitly retained in the verdict.

Infrastructure, provider transport/contract failures, stale/adoption errors, source mismatch, uncounted sends, retries, missing records or changed goldens yield INVALID evidence. A cap stop, incomplete trial or exhausted budget alone cannot establish NO_EFFECT. A clear NO_EFFECT verdict requires valid evidence of repeatable loss or absence of improvement within the agreed scope. Otherwise report EFFECT_NOT_DEMONSTRATED and the concrete remaining boundary.

R independently evaluates complete recorded requests, closed responses, gate choices/confidence/stops, selected/proof traces, initial/final semantic records, DecisionLogs, browser lifecycle and the reconciled public actual-send journal. Source CI or source review alone proves no drawing effect. All conclusions remain scoped to this small PoC.
